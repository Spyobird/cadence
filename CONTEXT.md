# Cadence

A personal tool for committing to two goals each quarter and reflecting on them daily. One person, one phone.

## Language

**Quarter**:
A calendar quarter (Jan–Mar, Apr–Jun, Jul–Sep, Oct–Dec). The unit of commitment; its length is 90–92 days.
_Avoid_: Cycle, 90-day block, season

**Quest**:
One of the two goals — **Life** or **Work** — committed to for a Quarter, written using the scaffold (main quest, why it matters, success metrics, commitments, why it's exciting).
_Avoid_: Goal, objective

# Cadence

A personal tool for committing to two goals each quarter and reflecting on them daily. One person, one phone.

## Language

**Quarter**:
A calendar quarter (Jan–Mar, Apr–Jun, Jul–Sep, Oct–Dec). The unit of commitment; its length is 90–92 days.
_Avoid_: Cycle, 90-day block, season

**Quest**:
One of the two goals — **Life** or **Work** — committed to for a Quarter, written by completing the Scaffold. Every Quarter has exactly one of each. Its parts, in order: Main Quest, Why it matters, Success Metrics, Why it's exciting, Obstacle, Commitments.
_Avoid_: Goal, objective

**Scaffold**:
The fixed first-person sentence openings ("My Life Main Quest is to …") that a Quest is written by completing, so that it reads back as a short vision statement.
_Avoid_: Template, form

### Parts of a Quest

**Main Quest**:
The one-sentence aim that heads a Quest ("My Life Main Quest is to …"). One part of the Quest, not the whole of it.
_Avoid_: Aim, headline, title

**Why it matters**:
Why the Quest is the single most important thing to accomplish this Quarter: the reason.
_Avoid_: Importance

**Success Metric**:
An observable, verifiable result that will be true by the Quarter's end. An outcome, not an action.
_Avoid_: Completion criterion, observable result

**Why it's exciting**:
What makes the Quest personally compelling: the pull, as distinct from the reason.
_Avoid_: Excitement

**Obstacle**:
What is most likely to get in the way of the Quest, ideally with the planned response for when it shows up.
_Avoid_: Friction, blocker

**Commitment**:
An action or habit within the owner's control that makes the Success Metrics happen, ideally with a when. An input, not a result.
_Avoid_: Step, task
